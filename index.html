FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kinds of automations can you build?

Almost anything repetitive: scrapers, outreach systems, AI agents, custom bots, API integrations, and data pipelines. If you can describe the manual process, I can usually automate it.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Everything I build is custom and handed over to you — no lock-in, no proprietary platform holding your automation hostage.

Can it run on a schedule / on autopilot?

Yes. Most of what I build is designed to run unattended — scheduled, monitored, and alerting you only when it needs attention.

What about sites that block scrapers?

I handle anti-blocking with proxy rotation, request throttling, and resilient parsing, and build in monitoring so breakages get caught fast.

How do we get started?

Send a message describing the process you want automated. I'll reply with an approach, rough scope, and timeline.

Do you maintain automations after building them?

Yes — I offer ongoing support and maintenance so your automations keep working as sites and tools change.
